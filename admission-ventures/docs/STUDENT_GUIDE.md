# Student guide

1. **Sign up** with your name, email, Bangladeshi mobile number and a password (8+ characters, with a letter and a number). Confirm your email, then log in. Forgot your password? Use “Forgot password”.
2. **Find a course** under Courses. Free preview lessons can be watched before you buy.
3. **Enrol**: press Enrol, then Pay. You are taken to the secure payment page. Access opens automatically after the payment is confirmed. If the confirmation is slow, wait: the page keeps checking. Never pay twice; check **Orders** first.
4. **Learn**: open My courses, choose a lesson, watch, download the PDF if there is one, and press *Mark as complete*.
5. **Exams**: open Exams. The timer runs on the server clock, so changing your phone's clock does nothing. Your answers save as you choose them; if your connection drops, reopen the exam and continue. At zero the exam submits itself. Results and (when released) the answer review are under *Last result*.
6. **Problems?** Quote your order reference (starts with “AV-”) when contacting support.
